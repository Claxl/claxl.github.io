**STAR-Bench** is a benchmark we built to characterize on-board satellite
image registration. It spans a wide range of terrain types and difficulty
levels and includes roughly 24k registration tasks — enough to study how
accuracy, runtime, and energy trade off across feature-based, intensity-based,
and hybrid approaches.

The benchmark backs the SIGMETRICS 2026 paper and is the empirical foundation
for the QAC runtime metric used in UCHIHA.
